# Product Vision - Game Selection Process (EVOCR-8009)

## 1. Overview

The Game Selection Process is an EVO-powered capability within the IGT Charitable Gaming platform that enables authorized Charity / Licensed Organization Users to make quarterly game selections for Charitable Gaming operations. North Dakota is the first-market regulatory and process baseline for this capability, while the product direction is to preserve reuse for future Charitable Gaming markets where market-specific behaviour is approved and configured.

For each quarter, authorized Charity / Licensed Organization Users must be able to independently select game titles and applicable Cost Per Play options from those made available for the relevant Game Selection period. EVOCR-8009 covers selecting, reviewing and saving these choices; it does not define how selected games are distributed, downloaded, installed, enabled or otherwise made available on terminals.

This Product Vision is limited to the Game Selection Process. It does not define the full Charitable Gaming platform, detailed UX design, technical implementation, or requirements for other Charitable Gaming feature areas.

## 2. Business Overview

IGT is entering Charitable Gaming through an EVO-powered platform model that extends existing EVO, ILink, VLT EGM, IGT content and redemption capabilities. North Dakota is the first jurisdictional configuration and the primary regulatory and process baseline.

Within this operating model, the Game Selection Process supports the Charity / Licensed Organization’s quarterly decision about which game titles and Cost Per Play options should be available for the upcoming operating period. The capability is intended to provide a simple, task-focused and auditable self-service process that does not require technical platform knowledge.

Saved selections provide input to the wider EVO and ILink operating model. The Game Selection Process does not own the detailed deal lifecycle, game distribution, installation, terminal control, redemption or regulatory reporting processes.

Game Selection is accessed through the EVO browser-based experience, with laptop and tablet as the confirmed device baseline. Detailed navigation, screen design, interaction behaviour and technical data responsibilities remain to be defined in downstream artefacts.

## 3. Business Objective

The objective of the Game Selection Process is to establish an EVO-based business capability that enables authorized Charity / Licensed Organization Users to manage quarterly game and Cost Per Play selections for North Dakota Charitable Gaming operations. Key goals are to:

- Enable authorized Charity / Licensed Organization Users to select approved game titles and applicable Cost Per Play options for an upcoming quarterly period. 
- Provide a controlled, auditable, simple and task-focused self-service selection process through IntelligenEVO. 
- Support Charity / Licensed Organization operational independence through a self-service process that does not require technical platform knowledge.
- Quarterly Game Selections must be completed and recorded so that the selected games and Cost Per Play options are available for subsequent Charitable Gaming activities during the relevant operating period.
- Maintain a clear boundary between Game Selection and adjacent capabilities such as deal lifecycle, regulatory reporting, redemption, installation and device operation. 
- Use North Dakota as the confirmed first-market baseline while keeping reusable product capability distinguishable from jurisdiction-specific behaviour. 

## 4. Scope

This Product Vision covers the business scope for EVOCR-8009 Game Selection Process only.

### 4.1 Included in Scope

- Quarterly Game Selection through IntelligenEVO, using North Dakota as the first-market regulatory and process baseline. 
- Selection of approved game titles by authorized Charity / Licensed Organization Users. 
- Selection of approved Cost Per Play options where such options are available. 
- Review and saving of quarterly Game Selections. 
- Association of a selection with the applicable approved Charity / Licensed Organization, licensed site/location or other confirmed operating context, subject to OQ-014. 
- Browser-based access through EVO on a laptop or tablet. 
- Controlled access to the Game Selection capability because not all Charitable Gaming Users are expected to perform selection activities. 
- Traceability of quarterly selections and changes at a level sufficient to support operational and compliance review. 
- High-level business handoff of saved or finalized selections to the wider EVO and ILink operating model, without defining detailed downstream processing. 
- North Dakota-specific selection behaviour must remain distinguishishable from reusable Charitable Gaming product capability, while allowing for future market-specific adaptations where practical.

### 4.2 Excluded from Scope

- Full Charitable Gaming platform vision.
- Electronic Pull Tab deal lifecycle, including deal creation, commingling, replenishment, pre-fetch, no-deal behaviour and quarter-close processing.
- Mobile Validator and voucher-redemption processes.
- Regulatory report generation, reconciliation and reporting UX.
- Retailer Portal, which is tracked separately under EVOCR-8111 / EVOPROD-572.
-Detailed distribution, download, installation, game-readiness and field-deployment operations.
-Game or terminal enablement and disablement behaviour.
- iLink site-management and terminal-control functionality.
- EGM gameplay, outcome delivery, game presentation and voucher issuance.
- Detailed UI and UX design is outside the scope of this Product Vision and will be defined in the UX Design Requirements artefact.
- Detailed roles and permissions, validation logic, error handling, edge cases and acceptance criteria are outside the scope of this Product Vision and will be defined in the appropriate downstream artefacts.
- Technical architecture, APIs, data ownership, integration design and system-of-record decisions are outside the scope of this Product Vision and will be addressed through the appropriate downstream analysis and design work.
- Detailed market-specific or customer-specific requirements that are not confirmed for the North Dakota Game Selection baseline.

## 6. Constraints

The following constraints apply at Product Vision level. Detailed business rules, validations, acceptance criteria, and technical design should be defined in downstream artefacts.

### 6.1 Business / Regulatory Constraints

- North Dakota is the confirmed first-market compliance and process baseline for this feature.
- Authorized Charity / Licensed Organization Users must be able to independently select game titles and applicable Cost Per Play options from those made available for the relevant Game Selection period.
- The Game Selection Process must align with applicable North Dakota limits and controls for available Electronic Pull Tab game titles and selection options.
- Game Selection must align with the quarterly operating cycle so that selections are completed and recorded for the relevant operating period.
- Selected games and related selection choices must be traceable enough to support operational and compliance review.
- North Dakota-specific selection behaviour must remain separate from reusable Charitable Gaming product capability, with future market-specific adaptations considered where practical.

### 6.2 Product / Delivery Constraints

- This Product Vision is limited to EVOCR-8009 Game Selection Process and must not absorb the full Charitable Gaming platform scope.
- Game Selection is provided through the EVO browser-based experience, with laptop and tablet as the confirmed device baseline.
- Detailed UI and UX design is outside the scope of this Product Vision and will be defined in the UX Design Requirements artefact.
- Game Selection must fit the wider EVO-powered Charitable Gaming platform and use terminology consistent with the approved product strategy.
- Detailed User Stories, roles and permissions, validation logic, error handling, edge cases and acceptance criteria are outside the scope of this Product Vision and will be defined in the appropriate downstream artefacts.
- Open Questions must remain visible until resolved so downstream artefacts do not assume decisions that have not been made.

### 6.3 Dependencies

- Game Selection depends on game catalogue and game metadata sufficient to present the game titles and Cost Per Play options made available for selection.
- Game Selection depends on confirmation of whether each quarterly selection is maintained for a licensed site/location, for the Charity / Licensed Organization as a whole, or for another approved operating grouping.
- Game Selection depends on the quarterly operating lifecycle to determine when selections can be created, changed, completed and used by subsequent Charitable Gaming activities.
- Subsequent Charitable Gaming activities depend on completed and recorded Game Selections, but their detailed behaviour is outside this Product Vision.
- UX Design Requirements depend on confirmation of which Game Selection capabilities, if any, must support mobile-sized devices of approximately 6.3 inches.
